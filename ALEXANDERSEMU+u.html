Save states

Use the EmulatorJS in-game menu to save or load a state. Open the emulator menu while a game is running and choose its save-state or load-state option. These controls are provided by the emulator core, so availability varies by system.

Controls

A USB/Bluetooth gamepad is recommended. Open the emulator's settings/controls menu after it loads to check keyboard and controller mappings.

The launcher currently downloads EmulatorJS from the internet. GameCube needs a separate compatible emulator such as Dolphin and is not playable through the current EmulatorJS setup. Genesis support depends on the available EmulatorJS core build. MAME compatibility depends on the ROM set version and may require additional BIOS/device ROMs.

No commercial game ROMs or BIOS files are included. Use your own legally obtained files. Save states are handled by the emulator menu. GameCube is listed as planned but is not enabled because the current emulator does not support it.
